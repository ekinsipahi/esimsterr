What is an eSIM?

A chip already soldered into your phone that can hold a mobile plan downloaded over the internet. The QR code you scan is just the download instruction.

Two lines at once

Your normal SIM keeps handling calls, SMS and WhatsApp on your own number. The eSIM only carries data, so nothing about your identity changes.

When the clock starts

On first connection at your destination — not at purchase, not at install. Install at home on Wi-Fi and nothing is wasted.

One setting people forget. Turn Data Roaming ON for the eSIM line. It sounds wrong, but a travel eSIM is technically roaming on partner networks — with the setting off it will show bars and carry no data.
