Does my phone support eSIM?

Two things have to be true: the device supports eSIM, and it is carrier-unlocked. Here is how to check in ten seconds, plus the full list of {{ device_count }} supported models.

On iPhone

Dial *#06#. If an EID number appears, the phone supports eSIM. Or check Settings → Cellular for an "Add eSIM" option. iPhone XS and newer, except mainland-China models.

On Android

Settings → Network & internet → SIMs. If you see "Download a SIM" or "Add eSIM", you are set. Pixel 3 and newer, Galaxy S20 and newer, plus many Xiaomi, Motorola, Oppo and Huawei models.

Carrier lock

A phone locked to your home operator will refuse any other network, eSIM included. Ask your operator to unlock it — usually free once the device is paid off.
