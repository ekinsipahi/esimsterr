About

Travel data should cost what it costs

{{ site_name }} sells prepaid travel eSIMs. We buy wholesale data from the same carrier aggregators the well-known eSIM apps use, add a thin margin, and pass the rest to you. That is the entire business model.

Why we exist

Roaming is the obvious villain, but the eSIM apps that replaced it are quietly expensive too. A 10 GB plan that costs a few dollars wholesale routinely retails for four or five times that, because the money goes into app-install ads rather than into the data.

We took the opposite approach: no ad budget, no influencer codes, no artificial "sale" prices that are really the normal price. Every plan page shows the cost per gigabyte and per day, so you can compare us with anyone else in ten seconds.

What you actually get

  • Local networks. Your eSIM connects to real carriers at your destination, not to a slow reseller pipe.
  • Instant delivery. The QR code appears as soon as the payment clears, day or night.
  • No identity checks. An email address is all we need. No passport upload, no KYC.
  • No subscriptions. Every plan is one-off. Nothing renews behind your back.

How we handle your data

We never see your traffic. What we store is what selling and supporting an eSIM requires: your email, your orders, and the usage counter the network reports back to us. There is no tracking pixel farm on this site.
