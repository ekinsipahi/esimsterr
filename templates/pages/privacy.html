Privacy Policy

Last updated {% now "j F Y" %}

This policy explains what {{ site_name }} collects, why, and what we never touch. The short version: we need an email address to deliver an eSIM, and we do not look at your traffic.

What we collect

  • Your email address. Required — it is how the QR code and receipt reach you.
  • Order records. Which plan you bought, when, how much you paid, and the reference from the payment provider.
  • eSIM technical data. The ICCID we issued you, its activation status, the data counter the network reports, and the radio technology last seen. This comes from our connectivity partner, not from your device.
  • Basic security data. The IP address used to place an order, kept to detect payment fraud.
  • Optional profile data. A display name, if you enter one.

What we never collect

  • The content of your traffic — the sites you visit, your messages, your calls. We are a reseller of connectivity, and none of that reaches us.
  • Card numbers. Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe; we receive only a token and the outcome.
  • Identity documents. There is no KYC, no passport upload, no ID check.

Why we process it

To sell and deliver the eSIM you asked for (contract), to keep records for accounting and to prevent fraud (legal obligation and legitimate interest), and — only if you opt in — to send occasional product emails (consent). Transactional emails about your own orders are sent regardless of marketing preferences, because you need them.

Who we share it with

  • Our connectivity partner, to issue and manage the eSIM. They receive your email address and the plan you bought.
  • Stripe and NOWPayments, to take payment.
  • Our email provider, to deliver order and support email.
  • Our hosting and error-monitoring providers, which process data incidentally while running the site.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Cookies and analytics

We set a session cookie so the site can keep you signed in and remember a guest order, and a preference cookie for light or dark mode. If analytics is enabled, it runs with advertising signals switched off and IP anonymisation on.

How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept as long as accounting law requires. eSIM technical records are kept while the line is live and for a short period afterwards for support. If you delete your account we deactivate it and unlink your email; records required for accounting remain.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, or an export, and you can withdraw marketing consent at any time from the unsubscribe link or your account page. Email {{ support_email }} and we will action it.
