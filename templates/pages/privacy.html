{% blocktrans %}This policy explains what {{ site_name }} collects, why, and what we never touch. The short version: we need an email address to deliver an eSIM, we keep enough of a record to defend a payment dispute, and we do not look at your traffic.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What we collect" %}

  • {% blocktrans %}Your email address. Required — it is how the QR code and receipt reach you.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Order records. Which plan you bought, when, the subtotal, any discount applied, the amount actually charged, and the reference from the payment provider.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}eSIM technical data. The ICCID we issued you, its activation status, the data counter the network reports, and the radio technology last seen. This comes from our connectivity partner, not from your device.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Request details attached to an order. The IP address the order was placed from, the browser user agent, the browser language and the referring page. See the fraud-prevention section below.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Coupon and discount records. The code you entered, the order it applied to and the discount amount.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Subscription records, if you choose an auto-renewing plan: the billing identifiers our payment processor gives us, the price per cycle, the renewal date and the renewal history.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Support and assistant conversations, including anything you type into the chat assistant.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Optional profile data. A display name, if you enter one, and a name you give an eSIM line to tell it from another.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What we never collect" %}

  • {% trans "The content of your traffic — the sites you visit, your messages, your calls. We are a reseller of connectivity, and none of that reaches us." %}
  • {% trans "Card numbers. Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe; we receive only a token and the outcome." %}
  • {% trans "Identity documents. There is no KYC, no passport upload, no ID check." %}
  • {% trans "Location beyond the country of the network your eSIM registers on, which is simply the destination you bought." %}

{% trans "Fraud prevention and chargeback defence" %}

{% trans "Digital goods delivered in seconds are a standard target for stolen cards, and a guest order has no account behind it. So, when an order is placed, we record the IP address the request came from, the browser user agent string, the browser language and the referring page, and we store them against that order." %}

{% trans "We use that record for three things and nothing else: to score an order for payment fraud before we provision a line, to answer a bank or card scheme when a payment is disputed, and to trace abuse of coupons or of the network itself. It is never used for advertising, profiling or tracking you across other sites, and we do not sell or share it for those purposes." %}

{% trans "The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and in defending payment disputes, and, where card-scheme rules require it, our legal obligation. We keep this request data for 24 months from the order date and then delete it, which leaves a margin over the dispute windows the card schemes allow. The order itself, minus that request data, is kept for as long as accounting law requires." %}

{% trans "Coupons and promotions" %}

{% trans "If you use a discount code we store the code, the order it was applied to and the amount it took off. Where a code is limited to one use per customer, we also keep a redemption record linked to your email address or account so the limit can be enforced." %}

{% trans "This is part of processing your order, and the record stays with the order for the same period. Discount codes are not used to build a marketing profile, and entering one does not sign you up to anything." %}

{% trans "Auto-renewing subscriptions" %}

{% trans "A subscription needs an account, because a recurring charge needs somewhere you can see and stop it. For each subscription we store the customer and subscription identifiers our payment processor returns, the price per cycle, the renewal interval, the date of the next renewal, how many times it has renewed and whether it is set to cancel at the end of the period. The card itself stays with the payment processor." %}

{% blocktrans %}We process this to perform the contract you entered into. You can cancel at any time from your dashboard or from the billing portal; cancelling stops future renewals and does not refund the period already delivered. The full commercial terms are in the terms of service and the refund policy. After a subscription ends, the billing record is kept for accounting.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "The chat assistant" %}

{% trans "The assistant in your account is an AI model answering support questions with the context of your own orders and eSIM lines. Conversations are stored against your account so the thread makes sense next time you open it, and so a human can pick it up where the model left off." %}

{% trans "Our support team can read those conversations. We review them to resolve escalations, to check answer quality and to catch abuse. Messages are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate a reply; under their commercial terms they are not used to train their models." %}

{% trans "Please do not paste passwords, full card numbers, recovery codes or identity documents into the chat. We never ask for any of them, and the assistant does not need them to help you. If you post something sensitive by accident, email us and we will delete that conversation." %}

{% trans "Assistant conversations are kept while your account is open and are deleted with it, or sooner on request." %}

{% trans "Why we process it" %}

{% trans "To sell and deliver the eSIM you asked for and to run any subscription you started (contract), to keep records for accounting and to prevent and investigate fraud (legal obligation and legitimate interest), and — only if you opt in — to send occasional product emails (consent). Transactional emails about your own orders are sent regardless of marketing preferences, because you need them." %}

{% trans "Who we share it with" %}

  • {% blocktrans %}Our connectivity partner, to issue and manage the eSIM. They receive your email address and the plan you bought.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Stripe and NOWPayments, to take payment and, for subscriptions, to bill each renewal.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Anthropic, to generate chat assistant replies, where the assistant is enabled.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Our email provider, to deliver order and support email.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Our hosting and error-monitoring providers, which process data incidentally while running the site.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% trans "A bank, card scheme or payment processor investigating a disputed payment, limited to the evidence that dispute requires." %}

{% trans "We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers." %}

{% trans "Cookies and analytics" %}

{% trans "We set a session cookie so the site can keep you signed in and remember a guest order, a CSRF cookie that protects forms, and a cookie recording your chosen language. Your light or dark mode preference is not a cookie at all: it is stored in your own browser and never sent to us. If analytics is enabled, it runs with advertising signals switched off and IP anonymisation on." %}

{% trans "How long we keep it" %}

  • {% trans "Order, payment, coupon and subscription billing records: as long as accounting law requires." %}
  • {% trans "IP address, user agent, browser language and referrer attached to an order: 24 months from the order date." %}
  • {% trans "eSIM technical records: while the line is live and for a short period afterwards for support." %}
  • {% trans "Support tickets and assistant conversations: while your account is open, or sooner on request." %}

{% trans "If you delete your account we deactivate it and unlink your email; records we are required to keep for accounting remain, and are not used for anything else." %}
